## The complete job

A team takes requests in, turns an accepted brief into an executable project, assigns real owners and dates, coordinates dependencies and capacity, produces and reviews deliverables, obtains version-specific human approvals, and reports honestly to stakeholders against goals. A switching buyer brings active projects, history, files and permissions with them, connects their existing services and continues work without a silent loss of context. Agents do coordination and first drafts, carry approved work forward and show their sources; humans keep decisions and accountability.

This is one independent product. Workspaces are organizational divisions inside an owner-run business installation; a separate isolated business uses a separate installation. Reporting, workload, time tracking, unlimited guests and agent teammates are included, with no seat minimums or buckets. Difficulty, provider app approval, native mobile distribution and source export limitations are engineering obligations, never reasons to drop a clause. Dollar budgets, rates, margin and invoicing belong to Gauge; Pegboard's USD field is work metadata, not a billing ledger.

The evidence base is lane ct’s INVENTORY.md and inventory.json for Pegboard, researched on 20 October 2026. The desk inputs are TARGET.md, row.json and naming-only NAME.md for the work category. Uptime and Orbit's launch contracts set the operational depth; no behavior is asserted implemented by borrowing their tests.

## Acceptance conventions

Journeys use the actual deployed Cloudflare/Base product, its authenticated browser routes, versioned public API, installed mobile clients or documented import/CLI doors. Route names in prose are buyer-facing destinations to implement, not claims of existing paths. Every browser journey runs and is personally inspected at **1440 and 390 CSS pixels** with realistic US campaign, operations and agency data, keyboard and assistive-technology access. A phone-width web view does not prove native mobile support.

Provider tests use an authorized provider sandbox or a network-edge fake built from the provider's current official request, response, event and error schemas. Fakes never replace Pegboard's domain, permissions, persistence, queue or scheduling. Provider app registration, consent and real-account qualification remain required before claiming a live integration; a schema-fake result must be labeled as such. Delays, retries, quota limits, lost responses, revoked access, duplicates, concurrent edits and process restarts are exercised where relevant. Sending and provider acknowledgment never imply a human read, update, approval or completed deliverable. Human approval is required for published plans, client/executive publications, spend, new outside-organization disclosures and agent/AI-produced changes to other people's dates. A current Admin-reviewed deterministic rule activation under PEG-012 is already approval for its in-scope date and assignment actions, including dates set on newly created tasks; it does not need approval again on each run. The reviewed-share approval for ordinary system notices below is already human approval, not a per-message review requirement. The approval semantics below apply to every clause; no agent or rule can grant that approval to itself.


An **external send** is disclosure to a person or destination outside the organization: clients, vendors, public pages, guest recipients or externally shared channels, even when the transport is the company's Slack, Teams, Nook or mailbox. A company-only channel/person/mailbox is an internal destination, not external merely because its provider is SaaS. Internal task notifications, digests, reminders and rule/agent posts execute under a standing owner-approved policy naming allowed audience, content/scope, cadence and channels, plus current recipient permissions and preferences.

A person's reviewed guest invitation/share under PEG-019 is also approval for ordinary **system notices inside that share**: assignments, due-soon/overdue reminders, digests, comment/activity notices, proof/approval notices and reminders, and recipient-bound reply routes. These fixed system event templates deliver to that current guest's own inbox, verified email, personal chat destination or device under the guest's own channel/digest/follow/mute/DND preferences, like a member's notices, without another approval. This applies when a rule or follow-up process triggers the ordinary notice, but never turns arbitrary text supplied by a rule or agent into a system notice. Every queued delivery rechecks the current recipient, share, record, field/comment/proof visibility and preferences; revoked/expired shares cancel queued notices and reply authority, and internal-only material is omitted from the payload and aggregate counts. An explicitly reviewed migration share adoption can supply the same approval; imported inactive shares alone cannot.

The share does **not** approve agent-composed outbound messages (including a personalized chase to that same guest), client/executive status publications, posts or feeds to client-shared channels, new audiences or content outside the share. Those remain pending for exact human recipient/content/version review. Outside-share record content also requires a separately reviewed access/share change before delivery; a send approval alone never grants access. Changed/unknown audiences wait for review. Human approval of an invitation, share or reviewed update does not authorize an agent to change another person's dates, activate a scheduling rule or approve a person's work; deterministic rule authority comes only from PEG-012’s separate reviewed activation. The human composing/publishing an exact reviewed update or sending an invitation is the approving person for that action; there is no second approver unless configured by the organization.

For a human date edit, the authorized editor sees the entire knock-on change set, including affected owners, dates, capacity and conflicts. Dragging and releasing after that visible preview, or pressing Apply in another date editor, is that person's approval of the exact set; execution rechecks permission and record versions and records the editor. No second sign-off is needed. If any affected record is outside the editor's grant, the proposal waits for its authorized approver without revealing hidden work. An autonomous agent or AI-produced date/assignment value always proposes changes affecting others for a named person's exact approval; personal unlocked blocks can follow PEG-069's standing personal policy. A deterministic rule’s activated version can set, clear or shift start/due dates and assign in-scope new or existing tasks immediately under PEG-012’s reviewed scope, offset and calendar. Changed/inactive rule versions, out-of-scope cascades and effects outside the activating Admin’s current grant wait for an authorized approver; activation cannot approve AI/agent output or grant new access. All reversible schedule changes retain PEG-064 undo.
